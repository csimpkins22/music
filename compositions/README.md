# Grandaddy-Style MIDI Compositions

Five original instrumental compositions in the style of Grandaddy, designed as songs that could appear on their next album. Each is handcrafted with attention to the band's signature sonic elements: lo-fi warmth, analog synth textures, piano arpeggios, patient builds, and melancholy beauty.

## Track Listing

### 1. Dial Tone Lullaby (4:14)
**Key: C major | Tempo: 72 BPM | 4/4**

A quiet, intimate piece — primarily piano and synth, no guitar. The spiritual successor to "Underneath the Weeping Willow." Piano arpeggios ripple throughout over a warm synth pad bed, building from solo piano to full synth wash. The bridge strips everything back to just piano and pad — the most vulnerable moment.

**Channels:** Piano, Synth Pad, Synth Lead, Organ, Bass, Vocal Melody, Drums

### 2. The Machinery of Sunlight (3:40)
**Key: F major | Tempo: 92 BPM | 4/4**

The "single" — an upbeat, driving track in the style of "A.M. 180." Built around a catchy 2-bar synth riff that defines the song, paired with crunchy power chords and a driving bass line. Clean guitar arpeggios in the verses, distorted guitar enters at the chorus. Includes a synth lead solo over the instrumental break.

**Channels:** Synth Pad, Synth Lead, Clean Guitar, Distorted Guitar, Bass, Vocal Melody, Drums

### 3. Beautiful Machines (5:49)
**Key: Eb major | Tempo: 84 BPM | 4/4**

The epic — inspired by "He's Simple, He's Dumb, He's the Pilot." Starts with 16 bars of solo piano, then accumulates layers one by one: synth pad, bass, drums, organ drone, synth arpeggio, clean guitar, distorted guitar wall, strings. Builds to a massive instrumental crescendo, then decays in reverse order until only the piano remains. The last sound is a single Eb.

**Channels:** Piano, Synth Pad, Synth Lead, Clean Guitar, Distorted Guitar, Organ, Bass, Strings, Vocal Melody, Drums

### 4. Last Signal Home (4:22)
**Key: G major | Tempo: 58 BPM | 3/4 (Waltz)**

A melancholy cosmic country waltz channeling the "Blu Wav" era. The only track in 3/4 time, featuring fingerpicked clean guitar, a pedal-steel-like synth melody with grace-note "bends," and a waltz-time drum pattern. The bridge uses a 5-chord progression (Am → Em → C → G → D) with the piano taking the lead. The most emotionally direct song.

**Channels:** Piano, Synth Pad, Pedal Steel Synth, Clean Guitar, Organ, Bass, Strings, Vocal Melody, Drums

### 5. Peripheral Glow (5:03)
**Key: D minor | Tempo: 70 BPM | 4/4**

A dreamy, atmospheric piece channeling "The Crystal Lake." Built on a sustained organ drone on D (the pedal tone) with a hypnotic 16th-note synth arpeggio providing constant motion. Uses a descending bass line progression (Dm → Dm/C → Bb → A) in the instrumental climax, evoking the "augmented climb" feel. Distorted guitar wall enters only during the extended instrumental section. Everything slowly dissolves back to the organ drone.

**Channels:** Piano, Synth Pad, Synth Arpeggio, Clean Guitar, Distorted Guitar, Organ Drone, Bass, Strings, Vocal Melody, Drums
