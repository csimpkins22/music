## Thematic Notes

The lyrics follow Grandaddy's signature themes:
- **Technology outliving its purpose** — lights left on, screens still burning, antennas still receiving
- **Suburban abandonment** — overgrown yards, empty buildings
- **Quiet ritual and observation** — driving past every evening, the compulsive need to check
- **Wistful rather than angry** — the glow is beautiful even though nobody needs it anymore

The word "peripheral" works double: it's at the edge of vision, and it's the abandoned peripheral device — the monitor, the antenna, the screen. The "glow" is both the literal phosphor burn-in and the residual warmth of something that used to matter.

Verse 3's arc (D5 falling to D4, silence, then C#→D homecoming) mirrors someone returning to a place they abandoned — hesitant, quiet, but ultimately finding the light still on.
