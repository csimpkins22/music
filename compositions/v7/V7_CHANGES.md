# Compositions v7 — Final Songwriter's Notes

Five iterations of handcrafted improvements from v2 demos to v7 finished compositions.
Each iteration: review → identify weaknesses → handcraft specific musical fixes → build → repeat.

## Iteration History

### v3: "The songwriter's ear" — Harmonic & melodic identity
- **Song 1**: Swapped Em for Am in verse; Ab major borrowed chord in chorus bar 7; falling 6th (G→B) signature hook; bridge uses Dm-Bb-Ab-G (two borrowed chords); strings enter only last 4 bars of final chorus; single high E5 as final note
- **Song 2**: Pre-chorus chromatic walk A→Bb→B→C; chorus 2 bar 7 substitutes Db; FINAL CHORUS MODULATES TO Gb MAJOR; synth riff transposition system
- **Song 3**: Chorus uses Fm-Db-Ab-Eb (borrowed Db); Cb major in crescendo climax; 2-bar near-silence before crescendo; "victory theme" Bb→Eb motif; single Eb4 as final sound
- **Song 4**: Bridge uses F major (borrowed from mixolydian); final chorus adds 2 bars of E MAJOR (parallel major surprise); organ pitch wobble in outro; last sound = single fingerpicked G
- **Song 5**: Chorus C→A7 substitution (C# leading tone); 2 bars total silence before chorus 2; Neapolitan Eb chord in instrumental; Picardy third ending (D minor→D major); rising D-F-A motif; organ dissolving to ±400 cents

### v4: "The arranger's pass" — Fixing structural weaknesses
- Melody leaps replacing stepwise motion (5ths, 6ths, octave drops)
- Chromatic bass walks between chords
- Synth pad inversions (voice separation from piano)
- Progressive drum builds (not identical patterns for 16 bars)
- True counter-melodies (contrary motion, not parallel harmony)
- Verse 2 melodies differentiated from verse 1
- Earned modulations via V7 preparation chords

### v5: "The detail pass" — Rhythmic precision & micro-moments
- Ghost snares and syncopated ghost kicks for pocket
- Chromatic approach notes in bass on beat 4
- Textural drops (synth arp removal) with compensating ghost notes
- Kick displacement in crescendo sections
- Piano-vocal interaction (spotlight moments)
- Counter-melody inversions in instrumental motifs

### v6: "The production pass" — Dynamic arcs & density management
- Instrument drop-outs to thin muddy sections (lead out of chorus 2)
- Bridge dynamic dips before final chorus (breathing room)
- Bass thinning synchronized with pad drop-outs (true space)
- Piano voice-leading chromatic grace notes mirroring bass
- Pre-chorus micro-silences (all instruments breathe together)
- Synth arp leading-tone emphasis on dominant chords
- Velocity fade for instrument handoffs (not abrupt cutoffs)

### v7: "The mastering pass" — Endings, transitions, final moments
- **Song 1**: Piano E5 vel lowered to 22 (smooth with pad vel=16); 2 extra bars silence after final note for breathing room
- **Song 2**: Single Gb2 bass landing on final bar; sharpened snare peak curve (concentrated at bar 47)
- **Song 3**: Crescendo final snare hit vel=68 (loudest percussion in song); piano simplification bridge (arpeggios → sustained chords)
- **Song 4**: Final chord changed from G add9 to G major (definitive home); guitar final note extended to ring through an extra bar
- **Song 5**: Outro organ wobble returns to ±50 cents on final bar (signal returns to earth); bass D2 anchor added to final 4 bars

## Song Summaries

### 01 - Dial Tone Lullaby
**Key**: C major | **Tempo**: 72 BPM | **Duration**: ~4:50
Signature: Falling 6th interval (G→B) as melodic hook. Ab major borrowed chord surprise in chorus. Bridge uses Dm-Bb-Ab-G (two borrowed chords from C minor). Ends on single E5 piano note — the resolution of all those falling 6ths, now standing alone.

### 02 - The Machinery of Sunlight
**Key**: F major → Gb major | **Tempo**: 92 BPM | **Duration**: ~3:50
Signature: Key modulation in final chorus (half-step up). Pre-chorus chromatic ascent. Synth riff with key transposition. Verse melodies built on 5th intervals. Final bass landing on Gb2.

### 03 - Beautiful Machines
**Key**: Eb major | **Tempo**: 84 BPM | **Duration**: ~6:07
Signature: 16-bar instrumental crescendo with kick displacement. Db borrowed chord and Cb major (Neapolitan) at climax. 2 bars of near-silence before crescendo. "Victory theme" motif (Bb→Eb). Ends on solo Eb4.

### 04 - Last Signal Home
**Key**: G major | **Tempo**: 58 BPM | **Time**: 3/4 waltz | **Duration**: ~4:33
Signature: E major surprise in final chorus (parallel major — G# where G natural lived all song). Pedal steel on G# over E major. Bridge with borrowed F chord. Organ wobble escalating in outro. Ends on single fingerpicked G — home.

### 05 - Peripheral Glow
**Key**: D minor | **Tempo**: 70 BPM | **Duration**: ~5:16
Signature: A7 dominant substitution in chorus (C# leading tone). 2 bars total silence before chorus 2. Neapolitan Eb chord in instrumental. Rising D-F-A motif climbing octaves. Organ dissolving to ±400 cents then returning to earth. Picardy third ending (D minor → D major). Solo D5 piano as final sound.
